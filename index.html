    Good to know

    • Your PDF stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded.
    • If a question is cut badly, press Whole page in the toolbar to see the full page.
    • Scanned papers with no selectable text open in booklet view, with the answer sheet pinned underneath.
    • Shortcuts: Alt+N next, Alt+P previous, Alt+F flag, Alt+V navigator, Alt+E end exam, letter keys to answer.

    Engineering and Science Admissions Test (ESAT)

    Instructions

    Practice test

    • There is one question per screen. Choose one answer for each question. Wrong answers don't lose marks.
    • Select Flag for Review to mark a question to come back to. Navigator lists every question as Complete, Incomplete or Unseen.
    • With a mouse, right-click an option to cross it out.
    • Color Scheme changes the colours of the question area.
    • When time runs out, your answers are submitted automatically. You need your own paper for working. Calculators aren't allowed.

    Select Next to begin. The timer starts then.
