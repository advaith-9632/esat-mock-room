    Good to know

    • Your PDF stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded.
    • If a question is cut badly, press Whole page in the toolbar to see the full page.
    • Scanned papers with no selectable text open in booklet view, with the answer sheet pinned underneath.
    • Shortcuts: Alt+N next, Alt+P previous, Alt+F flag, Alt+R navigator, letter keys to answer.

    Instructions

    Before you begin

    • There is one question per screen. Choose one answer for each question; there is no penalty for wrong answers.
    • Use Flag for Review to mark questions to come back to. Navigator shows which are complete, incomplete or flagged.
    • Click ✕ beside an option to cross it out while you work.
    • The timer starts when you press Start Test. When time runs out, your answers are submitted.
    • You need your own paper for working. Calculators aren't allowed.
